              How the Apple Watch HR Bridge Works
            

            

              Because iOS Safari cannot read HealthKit directly, a 4-action native iOS Shortcut named SyncWatchHR grabs your Apple Watch Heart Rate & Active Calories from the last 60 minutes and either passes them right back into this web app via URL parameters OR posts them straight to your Google Sheet webhook!
            

          


          

            

              Step 1 • Find Heart Rate Samples
              
Open the Shortcuts app on iPhone → Create New Shortcut named SyncWatchHR. Add action "Find Health Samples": Type = Heart Rate, Start Date = is in the last 1 hour.

            


            

              Step 2 • Calculate Avg & Max HR
              
Add "Calculate Statistics" → Calculate Average of Health Samples (Save to variable AvgHR). Duplicate action for Maximum (Save to variable MaxHR).
